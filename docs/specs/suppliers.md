# Suppliers

## What It Does

The Suppliers page is a directory of the businesses you buy your materials from. Linking a supplier to a material creates a direct connection between what you buy, who you buy it from, and what it costs — making it easier to track costs, reorder when stock is low, and see the impact of supplier price changes.

---

## Views

Toggle between:
- **Table view** — compact rows for managing a large supplier list
- **Grid view** — card-based layout with contact info visible at a glance

---

## Supplier Information

Each supplier record stores:

- **Name** — the business or person's name
- **Contact name** — who to get in touch with
- **Email** — direct contact address
- **Phone** — optional phone number
- **Website** — link to their online store or catalogue
- **Location / Country** — where they're based (useful for estimating lead times)
- **Notes** — any free-form notes about this supplier

---

## Linking Suppliers to Materials

From any material in your Materials Library, you can link it to a supplier. This means:

- Each material card shows which supplier it comes from
- From a supplier's record, you can see which of your materials you source from them
- When you restock a material, you know exactly who to contact

---

## Actions

- **Add** a new supplier
- **Edit** supplier details
- **Delete** a supplier (only if no materials are linked, or with confirmation)
- **Bulk delete** multiple suppliers

---

## Search & Filtering

A search bar filters suppliers by name in real time.
