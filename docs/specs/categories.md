# Categories

## What It Does

Categories let you organise your products into groups — for example, "Candles", "Wax Melts", "Gift Sets". Once products are categorised, you can filter your product list by category, and the Home dashboard shows a breakdown of your top categories by volume.

---

## Managing Categories

The Categories page (`/categories`) lists all your categories with the number of products assigned to each.

**You can:**
- **Add** a new category by clicking the Add button and entering a name
- **Rename** a category by clicking the edit icon
- **Delete** a category (only if no products are currently assigned to it, or with a confirmation that products will be un-categorised)
- **Bulk delete** multiple categories at once

---

## Assigning a Category to a Product

Categories are set on the Create or Edit Product screen, via a dropdown. You can also bulk-reassign categories from the Products list using the bulk actions menu.

---

## Category Filtering

On the Products page, you can filter your list by category to see only products in that group. This is useful when you want to review pricing consistency across a product line.

---

## Category Analytics

The Home dashboard shows a **Top Categories** widget (a donut chart) breaking down your product portfolio by category. This gives you a quick read on where your volume is concentrated.
