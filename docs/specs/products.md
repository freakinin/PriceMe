# Products — Product List

## What It Does

The Products page is your full product catalogue. You can search, filter, sort, and bulk-edit every product you've created. It also gives you a live view of each product's pricing health — profit, margin, markup, and how your price stacks up against competitors.

---

## Views

Switch between two layouts at any time:

- **Table view** — a compact spreadsheet-style list, great for managing lots of products quickly
- **Grid view** — card-based layout, useful when you want to scan product names and prices visually

---

## Columns

The table includes these columns by default. You can hide any column you don't need and drag them into the order that works for you. Preferences are saved automatically.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Name** | Product name |
| **Category** | Which category this product belongs to |
| **Status** | Draft / In Progress / On Sale / Inactive |
| **SKU** | Stock-keeping unit code |
| **Product Cost** | Total cost to make one unit |
| **Price** | Your selling price |
| **Profit** | Price minus cost |
| **Margin %** | Profit as a percentage of the selling price |
| **Markup %** | Profit as a percentage of cost |
| **Market Position** | How your price compares to tracked competitors (shown as below / at / above market) |

---

## Searching & Filtering

- **Search bar** — instantly filters by product name as you type
- **Advanced filters** — add one or more column filters using operators like "contains", "equals", "starts with", and "ends with". Status filters support "equals" / "does not equal". Active filters appear as badges you can dismiss individually.

---

## Bulk Actions

Select any number of products and act on all of them at once:

- **Delete** — removes selected products (with confirmation)
- **Change status** — move a batch to Draft, In Progress, On Sale, or Inactive
- **Change category** — reassign a batch to a different category

---

## Inline Editing & Pricing

Each product row has a live pricing calculator. You can update the price and pricing method (Markup %, Set Price, Target Profit $, Target Margin %) directly in the table, without opening a separate edit screen. Profit, margin, and markup numbers update in real time.

**Fee-Aware mode** — toggle this on to see profit and margin after platform fees (Etsy, Shopify, or whichever fee profiles you've set up in Settings). Hover over the adjusted figures to see the fee breakdown.

---

## AI Product Generator

The "Generate with AI" button opens a guided form where you describe what you're making. PriceMe uses AI to suggest materials, labor times, shipping costs, and a recommended price — giving you a realistic starting point instead of a blank form. Available on Starter plan and above.

---

## Product Actions (per row)

- **Edit** — opens the full edit screen for that product
- **Duplicate** — creates a copy of the product (useful for similar items)
- **Save as template** — saves this product's cost structure as a reusable template
- **Delete** — removes the product (with confirmation)

---

## Product Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Being set up — costs and pricing aren't finalised yet |
| **In Progress** | Costs are entered; being priced before listing |
| **On Sale** | Actively listed for sale; appears on the On Sale page |
| **Inactive** | No longer being made or sold; kept for reference |

---

## Export

Click **Export** to download your full product list as a CSV file, including all cost and pricing figures.

---

## Plan Limits

| Plan | Products |
|------|---------|
| Free | Up to 5 products |
| Starter | Up to 10 products |
| Growth | Up to 30 products |
| Pro | Unlimited |

When you're close to your limit, a warning appears. Hitting the limit prevents new products from being created until you upgrade or delete existing ones.
