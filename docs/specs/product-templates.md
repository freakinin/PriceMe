# Product Templates

## What It Does

Templates let you save a product's cost structure as a reusable starting point. The next time you make something similar, you load the template and get all the materials, labour activities, other costs, and pricing settings pre-filled — so you're adjusting, not starting from scratch.

---

## When to Use Templates

Templates are most useful when you make the same type of product repeatedly, perhaps with minor variations each time:

- A candle template with your standard wax, wick, and fragrance quantities
- A print template with paper cost and packaging already filled in
- A jewellery template with your typical labour time and metal quantities

---

## Saving a Template

There are two ways to create a template:

**From an existing product:**
On the Products list, click the actions menu on any product and select **Save as Template**. A dialog asks you to give the template a name. All costs, pricing method, batch size, and variant structure are saved.

**From the Create/Edit Product screen:**
While building or editing a product, click **Save as Template** from the form toolbar.

---

## Using a Template

On the Create Product screen, click **Load Template** (or use the template picker shown when the form is empty). A dropdown lists all your saved templates. Select one and the form is populated with:

- Materials and their quantities
- Labour activities, times, and rates
- Other costs
- Default batch size and pricing method

You can then adjust anything before saving — the template is just a starting point, it doesn't lock anything in.

---

## Managing Templates

Templates are listed in Settings (or a dedicated Templates section). From there you can:

- **Rename** a template
- **Delete** a template you no longer need
- **View** what a template contains before using it

Deleting a template does not affect any products that were previously created from it.

---

## What a Template Stores

| Field | Included |
|-------|---------|
| Materials (name, quantity, unit) | Yes |
| Labour activities and rates | Yes |
| Other costs | Yes |
| Batch size default | Yes |
| Pricing method | Yes |
| Variant structure | Yes |
| Category | Yes |
| Target price | No (prices are product-specific) |
