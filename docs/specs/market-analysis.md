# Market Analysis & Competitor Tracking

## What It Does

Market Analysis shows you how your prices sit relative to what others are charging for similar products. By tracking competitor listings, PriceMe helps you spot whether you're priced too low (leaving money on the table) or too high (potentially losing sales), and gives you AI-generated recommendations for where to position your price.

---

## Market Position Column

On the Products page, a **Market Position** column shows each product's competitive standing at a glance:

- **Below market** — your price is lower than the average competitor price
- **At market** — your price is in line with competitors
- **Above market** — your price is higher than the average competitor price

This column is powered by the competitor data you've added to each product.

---

## Adding Competitors to a Product

From the product edit screen or the Market Analysis panel, you can add competitor listings for any product:

- Paste a competitor's product URL or enter their price manually
- Give the competitor a name or label
- PriceMe stores the price and checks it periodically for changes

**Plan limits apply:**

| Plan | Competitors tracked |
|------|-------------------|
| Free | 1 per product |
| Starter | 3 per product |
| Growth | 50 total |
| Pro | Unlimited |

---

## Market Analysis Panel

When viewing or editing a product, the Market Analysis panel shows:

- **Average competitor price** — the mean price across all tracked competitors
- **Competitor count** — how many you're tracking for this product
- **Your position** — below / at / above market
- **Price range** — the lowest and highest competitor prices
- **AI recommendations** — generated by Google Gemini, based on your margin and market position

---

## Price History

For each tracked competitor, PriceMe records price changes over time. You can see when a competitor's price went up or down, which helps you understand trends and anticipate market shifts.

---

## AI-Powered Insights

The Market Analysis panel includes AI insights that consider both your cost data and the competitive landscape:

- Whether your current price is likely to convert well
- How raising or lowering your price might affect perceived value
- Seasonal patterns in competitor pricing (when enough data is available)

---

## Automated Monitoring

PriceMe checks competitor prices automatically every 24 hours for all tracked competitors. If a significant price change is detected, you'll see it flagged on the Market Analysis panel. Notifications for price changes are coming in a future update.
