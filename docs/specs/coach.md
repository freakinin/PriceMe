# AI Coach

## What It Does

The Coach is PriceMe's built-in pricing advisor. It analyses your product catalogue, cost structure, and sales data — then gives you personalised advice on where you might be under-pricing, which products have the healthiest margins, and what you could do to grow your revenue. You can also chat with it directly to ask pricing questions in plain language.

The Coach is powered by Google Gemini AI and works best once your cost data and Coach profile are filled in.

---

## Setting Up Your Profile

Before the Coach can give you relevant advice, it needs to know a bit about your business. You'll be prompted to answer five questions:

1. What type of products do you make?
2. Where do you sell?
3. How long have you been selling?
4. What's your biggest pricing challenge?
5. What's your monthly revenue goal?

You can set this up during post-signup onboarding or by visiting the Coach page for the first time.

---

## Business Health Score

A score from 0 to 100 gives you an at-a-glance read on your overall pricing health. It's calculated from factors like:

- How many of your products have complete cost data
- Whether your margins are sustainable
- How your prices compare to the market
- Progress towards your revenue goal

The score also appears as a widget on your Home dashboard.

---

## Pricing Insights

The Coach generates specific insights about your products — not generic tips, but observations based on your actual numbers:

- "Your candles have a 12% margin. At your volume, raising the price by £1.50 would still be competitive and add £45/month in profit."
- "3 of your products have no labour cost entered — your margins may be overstated."
- "Your wax cost has increased significantly. Consider reviewing these 4 products."

Insights are generated on demand or refreshed automatically when your product data changes.

**Plan limits apply:** Free accounts get a small number of insights. Starter and above get more. Growth and Pro have no limit.

---

## Chat

Ask the Coach anything about your pricing strategy in natural language:

- "Am I charging enough for my soy candles?"
- "How does my margin compare to typical Etsy sellers?"
- "What would happen to my profit if I raised prices by 10%?"

The Coach has context about your products and settings, so answers are grounded in your actual numbers, not generic advice.

Chat conversations are saved so you can refer back to them. Each conversation is a separate session.

**Plan limits apply:** Free accounts cannot use chat. Starter and above have unlimited chat.

---

## Reports

Generate a written analysis report covering your whole business or a specific product. Reports include:

- Product profitability breakdown
- Margin analysis and benchmarks
- Recommendations and next steps

Reports are generated on demand and saved for you to review later.

**Plan limits apply:** Starter gets 2 reports/month. Growth and Pro have no limit.

---

## Growth Chart

A widget on the Home dashboard (and on the Coach page) shows your revenue and profit trend over time, with a line showing progress towards your monthly goal.
