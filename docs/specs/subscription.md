# Subscription & Plans

## What It Does

PriceMe has four plan tiers. The plan you're on determines how many products you can create, how many competitors you can track, and how much of the AI Coach you can use. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from Settings.

---

## Plans

| Feature | Free | Starter | Growth | Pro |
|---------|------|---------|--------|-----|
| **Price** | Free | ~$11/mo | ~$25/mo | ~$36/mo |
| **Products** | 5 | 10 | 30 | Unlimited |
| **Competitors tracked** | 1 | 3 | 50 | Unlimited |
| **Coach insights** | 3 | 5/month | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Coach chat/day** | None | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Reports/month** | None | 2 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **AI product generator** | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Export to CSV** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Annual billing is available at a reduced rate.

---

## How Limits Work

When you're close to a limit (80%+), a warning appears in the relevant part of the app. When you hit the limit:

- A dialog explains which limit you've reached and offers a direct path to upgrade
- The blocked action (e.g. adding a product) is prevented until you upgrade or free up space

Limits are enforced consistently — both in the interface and at the API level.

---

## Upgrading

Click **Upgrade** from the limit warning, from Settings → Subscription, or from the plan comparison in Settings. Choose your new plan and confirm. The change takes effect immediately.

Stripe billing integration is coming in a future release. Until then, plan changes are self-service within the app.

---

## Downgrading

You can switch to a lower-tier plan from Settings → Subscription. If your current usage exceeds the new plan's limits (e.g. you have 15 products but are downgrading to a plan with a 10-product limit), you'll be asked to remove the excess before the change is processed.

---

## Free Trial

New accounts may receive a free Pro trial period. A banner in Settings and in the app header shows how many days remain. When the trial expires, your account moves to the Free plan automatically.

---

## Usage View

Settings → Usage gives you a detailed view of your current usage across every plan limit — products, competitors, insights, chat, and reports — each shown as a progress bar.
